How It Began

A Beagle Named Duffy Changed Everything

In 2024, our founder Mohit was searching for truly premium food for his beagle Duffy. Every store carried the same generic options. Nothing felt good enough.

So he built Petify — a platform where every single product is hand-selected, vet-reviewed, and worth your trust. No compromises, no fillers, no shortcuts.

"If I wouldn't give it to Duffy, it doesn't go on our shelves."

Today, Petify is India's most trusted luxury pet boutique — serving over 15,000 pet families across every city.

Our Core Values

🩺

Vet-Approved Always

Every product on Petify is reviewed and approved by certified veterinarians. Your pet's health is never a marketing claim — it's our baseline.

🌿

100% Natural Ingredients

No artificial preservatives, no hidden fillers. We work directly with ethical suppliers who share our belief that pets deserve clean, honest nutrition.

Luxury Without Guilt

Premium quality shouldn't cost the earth. We price fairly, ship free above ₹999, and make luxury accessible to every pet parent in India.

Hassle-Free Returns

If your pet doesn't love it, we don't want you to keep it. Our 30-day no-questions-asked return policy is as real as it sounds.

Community First

We partner with animal shelters across India, donating 2% of every order to rescue and rehabilitation programs for stray animals.

Pan-India Delivery

From Kashmir to Kanyakumari — fast, insured, and carefully packed deliveries so every order arrives exactly as it should.
